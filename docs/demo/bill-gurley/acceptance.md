# Real-video acceptance: Bill Gurley TED talk

Source: <https://youtu.be/z-WfDn_0AWE>

This acceptance case comes from the project owner's real V2 output. It exists to prevent structural validation from approving weak editorial material.

## Pack 01 — fascination as a mechanism for sustained learning

| Role | Source | Publish Chinese |
|---|---|---|
| hook | Obsessive and continuous learning is not an input — it's an output. It's not the cause, it's the effect. | 持续而近乎痴迷的学习，不是前提，而是着迷之后的结果。 |
| direction | You should follow your fascination. | 去追随真正让你着迷的东西。 |
| contrast | Passion doesn't invoke work. | 热情本身，并不会自动带来行动。 |
| mechanism | When you're fascinated, you study “automagically.” | 一旦真的着迷，你会不由自主地钻进去。 |
| felt result | When you learn about something you're fascinated by, you get energy. You want to smile. | 钻研真正着迷的东西，会让你越学越有劲，甚至忍不住开心。 |

Rejected: `The learning comes for free. Zero conscious effort.` repeats the mechanism and previously produced the unnatural slogan `学习自己会发生，零刻意努力`.

## Pack 02 — AI widens the gap between learning and avoiding learning

| Role | Source | Publish Chinese |
|---|---|---|
| hook | But what if the safe jobs aren't safe anymore? | 可那些所谓的“安全工作”，如果也不再安全呢？ |
| problem | Well-intentioned parents and advisors have pushed kids towards the safe jobs: medicine, legal, finance, comp sci. | 许多父母和顾问出于好意，把孩子推向医学、法律、金融和计算机这些“安全职业”。 |
| reframe | I don't think it's the jobs we love that are under threat. It's the ones people were ambivalent about already. | 我不认为先受威胁的是人们热爱的工作，而是那些本来就做得不上心的工作。 |
| split | Those that use LLMs to learn faster than ever — and those that use LLMs to skip learning altogether. | 有人用 LLM 学得更快，也有人用它彻底跳过学习。 |
| close | For these fascinated artisans, AI is a jetpack. They learn faster, they soar higher. | 对那些痴迷于自己手艺的人，AI 更像一只喷气背包：让他们学得更快，也飞得更高。 |

`Only 23 percent said yes.` is rejected because the survey question is absent. The `Those that ...` fragment must be expanded to a continuous source span before publication. `fascinated artisans` must retain the craft identity.

## Acceptance rules

1. A quote must be independently intelligible or expanded to a contiguous source span that supplies the missing referent or question.
2. Numerical specificity cannot compensate for missing context.
3. Every line must add a new proposition; role labels alone do not create progression.
4. Chinese is reviewed without the source first, then checked for source fidelity.
5. Additions, omissions, strengthenings, and weakenings are hard failures.
6. Bilingual cards default to five strips when six would reduce readability.
